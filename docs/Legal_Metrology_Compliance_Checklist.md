# Compliance Checklist — Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
*Extracted strictly from the uploaded Rules PDF (G.S.R. 202(E), 7th March 2011)*

---

## 1. Threshold Applicability (Rule 3) — Chapter II Exceptions

Chapter II ("Provisions applicable to packages intended for retail sale") does **not** apply to:
- (a) Packages containing quantity **more than 25 kg or 25 litre**, *excluding cement and fertilizer sold in bags up to 50 kg*.
- (b) Packaged commodities meant for **industrial consumers** or **institutional consumers**.
  - *Institutional consumer*: buys directly from the manufacturer for use by transportation, airways, railways, hotels, hospitals, or other service institutions.
  - *Industrial consumer*: buys directly from the manufacturer for use by that industry.

---

## 2. Mandatory Declarations on Every Retail Package (Rule 6)

Every package must bear a definite, plain, conspicuous declaration of:

| # | Required Declaration | Exception / Carve-out |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Name & address of manufacturer, or manufacturer **and** packer (if different); importer's name/address for imported packages | Food articles: Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954 applies instead (Explanation III) |
| (b) | Common/generic name of commodity; if multi-product package, name & number/quantity of each product | — |
| (c) | Net quantity in standard unit (weight/measure), or number if sold by number | — |
| (d) | Month & year of manufacture/pre-packing/import | Not required on: (i) bidi or incense-stick packages; (ii) domestic LPG cylinders of 14.2 kg or 5 kg marketed by a public sector undertaking. Also not required for food articles under PFA Act, or seeds labeled/certified under Seeds Act, 1966. Cosmetics follow Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945. |
| (e) | Retail Sale Price (MRP), inclusive of all taxes | Alcoholic beverages/spirituous liquor: State Excise Laws apply (unless they don't require RSP declaration, in which case these rules apply). No RSP declaration required on: (i) bidi packages; (ii) domestic LPG cylinders priced under the Administrative Price Mechanism. |
| (f) | Dimensions of commodity where sizes are relevant (and of each piece if different) | — |
| (g) | Such other matters specified in the rules | — |

**Explanation I:** If a name/address appears without "manufactured by"/"packed by," it is presumed to be the manufacturer's, and liability follows accordingly.

**Explanation II:** If a brand owner's name/address appears as marketer, the brand owner is held responsible for violations; where multiple names/addresses appear, prosecution is against the manufacturer listed first, not all of them.

**Additional Rule 6 requirements:**
- (2) Every package shall bear the name, address, telephone number, and e-mail (if available) of the person/office to contact for consumer complaints.
- (3) Individual stickers may **not** be affixed to alter/make a required declaration — *except* a sticker reducing the MRP (must not cover the original MRP declaration).
- (4) Stickers **are** permissible for declarations other than those required under these rules.
- (5) For commodities with components packed in 2+ units sold as a single commodity: declaration appears on the main package (with info about accompanying packages), or on each individual package with intimation on the main package; if sold as spare parts, all declarations go on each package.

## 3. Principal Display Panel (PDP) & Font Size (Rule 7)

- Package capacity ≤ 5 cubic cm: PDP may be a card/tape affixed to the package.
- Minimum numeral height (Table I, by net quantity in weight/volume):

| Net Quantity | Normal case | Blown/formed/molded/embossed/perforated |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 200 g/ml | 1 mm | 2 mm |
| Above 200–500 g/ml | 2 mm | 4 mm |
| Above 500 g/ml | 4 mm | 6 mm |

- Minimum numeral height (Table II, by net quantity in length/area/number):

| Net Quantity / PDP Area | Normal case | Blown/formed/molded/embossed/perforated |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 100 cm² | 1 mm | 2 mm |
| Above 100–500 cm² | 2 mm | 4 mm |
| Above 500–2500 cm² | 4 mm | 6 mm |
| Above 2500 cm² | 6 mm | 6 mm |

- Letter/numeral height minimum overall: 1 mm (2 mm if blown/formed/molded/embossed/perforated); width ≥ 1/3 of height (except numeral '1' and letters i, I, l).
- **Exception:** Sub-rules (1)–(3) of Rule 7 do **not** apply if the same information is already mandated by another law in force.

## 4. Where & How Declarations Must Appear (Rules 8–9)

- All declarations must appear on the PDP; area around the quantity declaration must be free of other printed information (space ≥ height of the numeral above/below; ≥ twice the numeral height left/right).
- **Exception:** Returnable soft-drink/beverage bottles — RSP may be indicated on the crown cap or bottle (or both), abbreviated as "MRP Rs..../₹....".
- Declarations must be legible, prominent; RSP and net quantity numerals must contrast conspicuously with the label background.
  - **Exception:** Info blown/formed/molded on glass or plastic need not be in a contrasting colour.
  - Handwritten/hand-script declarations are allowed if clear, unambiguous, legible.
- No declaration may require reading through a liquid commodity.
- Outer container/wrapper must repeat all required declarations — **except** when it is transparent and the inner package's declarations are easily readable through it, or when the inner package carries no declaration on its outer cover.
- Declarations must be in Hindi (Devnagri) or English — **may additionally** use any other language.

---

## 5. Name & Address of Manufacturer/Packer/Importer (Rule 10)

- Every package must conspicuously bear the complete name/address of manufacturer, or manufacturer + packer, or importer (for imported packages).
- **Exception:** Packages of capacity ≤ 5 cubic cm — sufficient if a mark/inscription enables the consumer to identify the manufacturer/packer/importer.
- Commodities manufactured outside India but packed in India: PDP must also show the packer's or importer's complete address in India.
- Name must be the actual corporate name, or (if unincorporated) the name under which business is conducted.

---

## 6. General Provisions on Quantity Declaration (Rule 11)

- Weight of wrappers/other materials excluded from net quantity.
- Where a commodity is not likely to vary in weight/measure due to environmental conditions: declared quantity must equal what the consumer actually receives — **no** "when packed" qualifier allowed.
- Where variation is negligible: declaration must account for the variation so the consumer receives not less than the declared quantity — again **no** "when packed" qualifier.
- **Exception:** Commodities likely to undergo *significant* variation due to environmental/other conditions **may** be qualified by "when packed," but only for the commodities listed in the **Third Schedule**:
  1. All kinds of soaps
  2. Lotions
  3. Cream (other than cream of milk)

---

## 7. Manner of Quantity Declaration (Rule 12)

- Declaration must use the most accurate/adequate unit: weight, measure, number, or combination.
- **Exception (Fourth Schedule):** commodities listed there follow prescribed units instead of the default weight/length/area/volume/number scheme of sub-rule (2), e.g.:
  - Aerosol Products — Weight
  - Acids in liquid form — Weight or volume
  - Compressed/liquefied gas (not LPG) — Weight and equivalent volume at stated temperature/pressure
  - Curd — Weight
  - Electric cables/wire, fencing wire — Length or weight / Number or weight
  - Fruits (all kinds) — Number or weight
  - Furnace oil, non-edible vegetable oil, edible oil/vanaspati/ghee/butter oil — Weight or volume
  - Heavy residual fuel oil — Weight; Industrial diesel fuel — Volume
  - Honey, malt-extract, golden syrup treacle — Weight
  - Ice cream & similar frozen products — Volume
  - Liquid chemicals — Weight or volume
  - Liquefied petroleum gas — Weight
  - Nails, wood screws — Number or weight
  - Paints (other than paste/solid paint), varnish, stains, enamels — Volume
  - Paste paint, solid paint — Weight
  - Rasgulla, Gulabjamun and similar sweet preparations — Weight
  - Ready-made garments — Number
  - Sauces (all kinds) — Weight
  - Tyres and tubes — Number
  - Yarn — Weight or length of yarn
  - Cosmetics (creams, shampoo, lotions, perfumes) — Weight or measure
- Where declaration by mass is used, manufacturer/importer/packer **may optionally** additionally declare the number of commodities in the package.
- No word/expression creating an exaggerated, misleading, or inadequate impression is allowed — e.g. "minimum," "not less than," "average," "about," "approximately" or similar are barred.
- **Exception:** Packages of capacity ≤ 5 cubic cm — quantity declaration may be on a tag/card/tape/similar device affixed such that it can't be removed without opening the container, bearing a mark identifying the manufacturer/packer.

## 8. Units of Weight/Measure/Number (Rule 13)

- Below 1 kg/1 m/1 m²/1 l → use gram / centimetre / square decimetre / cubic centimetre / millilitre as applicable.
- At or above 1 kg/1 m/1 m²/1 m³/1 l → use kilogram/metre/square metre/cubic metre/litre, with fractions expressed as decimals or sub-multiples.
- **Exception:** Where quantity equals exactly 1 kg/1 m/1 m²/1 dm³/1 m³/1 litre, it **may optionally** be expressed in the smaller sub-unit instead, at the option of the manufacturer/packer/importer.
- No number called dozen, score, gross, great gross, or the like may be specified on any package.
- Only International System of Units (SI) permitted for net quantity; items sold by number use symbol N or U.

---

## 9. Dimension & Sheet-Count Declarations (Rules 14–17)

- **Rule 14:** Bed-sheets, hemmed fabrics, dhoties, sarees, napkins, pillow-covers, towels, table cloths, etc. — must declare number and finished-size dimensions. If pieces differ in dimension, each piece's dimension and RSP must be separately declared **and** marked on each individual piece.
- **Rule 15:** Where dimensions/weight (or combination) relate to price, the quantity declaration must also include those dimensions/weight/combination.
- **Rule 16:** Sheet-type commodities (aluminium foil, facial tissues, waxed paper, toilet paper, etc.) — declare number of usable sheets **and** dimensions of each sheet.
- **Rule 17:** Container-type commodities (bags, boxes, cups, pans, etc.):
  - Bag-type: number of bags + linear dimensions, whether packaged in a perforated roll or otherwise.
  - Square/oblong/rectangular containers: number of containers + length, width, and (if required) depth.
  - Circular/round containers (not cups): number + diameter, and depth if necessary.
  - Where container use is tied to a standard weight/measure capability, that reference must be included in the quantity declaration.

## 10. Standard Package Sizes (Rule 5, Second Schedule)

- Commodities listed in the Second Schedule (baby food, weaning food, biscuits, bread, butter/margarine, cereals/pulses, coffee, tea, reconstituted beverages, edible oils/vanaspati/ghee, milk powder, non-soapy detergents, rice/flour/atta/rawa/suji, salt, soaps [laundry/non-soapy/toilet], aerated soft drinks, mineral/drinking water, cement, paints/varnish) must be packed only in the specified standard quantities.
- **Exception:** If packed in a non-listed size, the label must prominently declare *"Not a standard pack size under the Legal Metrology (packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011"* or *"non standard size under the Legal Metrology (packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011."*

---

## 11. Maximum Permissible Error (Rules 19, 22 & First Schedule)

- **Rule 22:** MPE per commodity is as per the First Schedule, accounting for (a) unavoidable weighing/measuring/counting deviations in good packaging practice; (b) variation from climate/transport/storage in good distribution practice; (c) variation due to packaging material/container.
- **First Schedule Table I** (MPE on net quantity by weight/volume) — declared quantity bands from "up to 50 g/ml" (9% error) down to "more than 15000 g/ml" (1.0% error), full table in the PDF.
- **First Schedule Table II** (MPE by length/area/number, for commodities not otherwise specified): 2% up to 10 m (length) / 1% thereafter; 4% up to 10 m² (area) / 1% thereafter; 2% for count.
- **Rule 19 test/approval criteria** — a lot is approved for sale only if **all** of:
  (a) statistical average of sample net quantity ≥ declared quantity;
  (b) deficiency error in **none** of the sample packages exceeds MPE;
  (c) each package bears the required declaration.
- **Exception (Rule 21(3) proviso):** Where a package bears the legend "when packed," **no punitive action** shall be taken against a retail/wholesale dealer if the officer is satisfied the deficiency is due to environmental conditions.

---

## 12. Wholesale Packages (Rule 24)

Every wholesale package must bear:
- (a) Name & address of manufacturer/importer, or packer (if not the manufacturer/importer);
- (b) Identity of the commodity contained;
- (c) Total number of retail packages contained, or net quantity in standard units.

**Exception:** Rule 24 does not apply to a wholesale package if a similar declaration is already required under any other law in force.

---

## 13. Export Packages (Rule 25)

- An export package cannot be sold in India unless the manufacturer/packer has re-packed or re-labeled it to comply with Chapter II — otherwise it is liable to seizure.

---

## 14. Overall Exemptions — Rule 26 (entire Rules do not apply to a package if)

- (a) Net weight/measure ≤ **10 g or 10 ml** (if sold by weight/measure).
  - **Proviso:** RSP and net quantity declarations are still required for packages of **10g–20g or 10ml–20ml**.
- (b) Fast food items packed by a restaurant/hotel and the like.
- (c) Scheduled and non-scheduled formulations covered under the Drugs (Price Control) Order, 1995 (Essential Commodities Act, 1955).
- (d) Agricultural farm produce in packages above 50 kg.

---

## 15. Registration Requirements (Chapter VI, Rules 27–30)

- Every manufacturer/packer/importer who pre-packs or imports a commodity must apply for registration (fee ₹500) with the Director/Controller.
  - Within 90 days of commencement of these rules (if already pre-packing/importing), or within 90 days of commencing such activity.
- Application must state: applicant's name; complete address of premises; name of commodity/commodities pre-packed/imported.
- Alteration to a registration certificate: fee ₹100.
- Registering authority must return incomplete applications within 7 working days, or register and grant a certificate if complete.
- **Rule 28 (exception/option):** Manufacturer/packer may apply to register a *shorter address* in addition to the complete address, if the Director/Controller is satisfied it is sufficient for consumer identification.
- Register of manufacturers/packers is open to public inspection free of charge (Rule 29(2)).

---

## 16. Penalties (Rule 32)

- Contravention of Rules 27–31 (registration/general chapter): fine of **₹4,000**.
- Contravention of any other provision with no punishment prescribed elsewhere in the Act/rules: fine of **₹2,000**.
- **Rule 33 — Power to relax:** Central Government may, after ascertaining genuineness of a compounding/court decision, permit a manufacturer/packer to pack for sale for a reasonable period by relaxing one or more provisions, subject to specified corrective measures.

---

## 17. Advertisement Requirement (Rule 31)

- Any advertisement mentioning the RSP of a pre-packaged commodity must also state the net quantity/number of the commodity in the package, in the **same font size** as the RSP.
